## What these protocols mean
- A machine-validation result means only that the checks actually performed against the published N.U standard passed or passed with limitations.
- Machine validation is **not** final producer approval, N.U team approval, Canon approval, rights clearance, or permission to publish.
- Human decisions are recorded and announced separately. They are not hidden inside a machine score.
- Every official archive record needs a stable ID, a reason for retention, a current state, and a retrieval path.
- Unresolved contradictions remain visible and are not silently merged or resolved by a model.
- A work that remains independent is still welcome in the independent-work discovery basket when the creator wants it listed and the public link and attribution can be checked.
- A work that is not accepted as an N.U reference is not erased or treated as worthless; it may remain part of the wider creative atmosphere without being represented as an official N.U reference.
