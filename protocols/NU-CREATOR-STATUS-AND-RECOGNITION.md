# N.U Creator Review, Status and Recognition Protocol
**Protocol ID:** NU-CREATOR-STATUS-001  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** PUBLIC DEVELOPMENT STANDARD

## 1. Four distinct states of judgment
Do not collapse these separate events:
1. **Machine validation:** checks conformity with the published N.U machine-readable standard.
2. **Producer decision:** the creator/producer reviews their own work and decides whether and where to publish it.
3. **N.U team decision:** an authorized team decision about whether the work is accepted as an N.U reference or official N.U publication.
4. **Public announcement:** a public record of the human decisions, where disclosure is authorized.

The machine is not the producer, the team, or the publisher. It must not claim any of their decisions.

## 2. Machine validation meaning
A result of PASS or PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS means only that the recorded checks passed to the stated extent. It does not mean final approval, endorsement, Canon, rights clearance, or permission to publish.

## 3. Producer decision
The producer is responsible for:
- Reviewing the full work and validation report.
- Confirming authorship/credits and rights for materials they supplied.
- Choosing independent publication or requesting the separate N.U team review route.
- Deciding whether their public work may be listed with its original URL and attribution.

A producer may publish independently. The work remains independent unless a separate human N.U decision confirms another status.

## 4. N.U team decision
An authorized N.U team review may accept or decline a work as an N.U reference or official N.U publication. The decision must identify the exact work/version and scope. A decline does not imply that the work has no creative value.

## 5. Public record of human decisions
Where disclosure is authorized, publish a concise announcement that records:
- Work ID, title, version, and public URL if published.
- Producer's decision and date, if the producer agrees to disclose it.
- N.U team decision, authorized role/team, date, and scope.
- Whether the work is accepted as an N.U reference, remains independent, or is not accepted as an N.U reference.
- A link to this protocol and the relevant work basket.

Do not publish private review notes, confidential messages, personal details, or unsupported claims about why a decision was made.

## 6. Recognition and independent-work atmosphere
- A creator's independent work may be listed and acknowledged when the creator wants it listed, the public URL is verified, attribution is appropriate, and rights/privacy checks pass.
- Independent publication is not a lesser or failed outcome.
- If a work is not accepted as an N.U reference or does not connect to the N.U network, do not mislabel it as official N.U material. It may still be part of the wider N.U creative atmosphere and may be recognized as an independent creator work.
- Do not discourage creators or imply that only large projects matter. A small idea or modest work can still be meaningful and influential.

## 7. Status vocabulary
Use explicit, non-interchangeable statuses:
- `DRAFT`
- `MACHINE_VALIDATION_PENDING`
- `MACHINE_VALIDATED`
- `MACHINE_VALIDATED_WITH_LIMITATIONS`
- `PRODUCER_REVIEW_PENDING`
- `PRODUCER_ACCEPTED_FOR_PUBLICATION`
- `PRODUCER_PUBLISHED_INDEPENDENTLY`
- `NU_TEAM_REVIEW_PENDING`
- `NU_REFERENCE_ACCEPTED`
- `NU_REFERENCE_NOT_ACCEPTED`
- `OFFICIAL_NU_PUBLICATION`
- `INDEPENDENT_WORK_LISTED`
- `WITHDRAWN`
- `SUPERSEDED`

Do not infer a status from silence, a machine result, or an unverified message.

## 8. Where to check
- General decisions and announcements: [N.U Public Announcements](https://github.com/niroouniverse-creator/NU-PUBLIC-BULLETINS/blob/main/boards/announcements.md).
- Published media articles: [Media Publications](https://github.com/niroouniverse-creator/NU-PUBLIC-BULLETINS/blob/main/boards/media-publications.md).
- Independent stories and works: [Independent Stories](https://github.com/niroouniverse-creator/NU-PUBLIC-BULLETINS/blob/main/boards/independent-stories.md).

## 9. Privacy and rights
Public records must use only the minimum necessary information. Never publish private contact details, confidential review notes, raw private drafts, or full copyrighted work without authorization.
